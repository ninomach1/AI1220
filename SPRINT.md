# Sprint 1 — StudyMatch

## Sprint Goal

By the end of Sprint 1, first-year students can find classmates in the same course, compare study goals and availability, and confirm a study session.

## Selected items

- [F] First-year students can find study partners who are taking the same course.
- [F] Students can compare potential partners' study goals and availability with their own.
- [F] Students can arrange and confirm a study session with matched partners.

## Definition of Done

- [ ] Every selected item has written acceptance criteria and all associated acceptance tests pass.
- [ ] Using test accounts, an independent tester can find only candidates taking the selected course and can compare their study goals and availability.
- [ ] Two matched test users can arrange and confirm a session, with the same confirmed details visible to both users.
- [ ] A student-council representative completes the documented Sprint 1 demonstration and records approval of the increment.
