# CampusEats: Product Backlog

## Product Description
A platform that reduces student wait times at the food court by enabling pre-ordering, real-time queue tracking, and estimated wait time visibility.

## Items
- [F] Students can view real-time food court queue lengths and estimated wait times
- [F] Food court staff can update queue status and meal preparation times
- [NF] App must work offline or with intermittent Wi-Fi (local caching, sync when connection returns)
- [F] Students can pre-order meals to bypass or reduce wait time
- [NF] System must provide queue updates with less than 2-minute latency
- [F] Users can reserve a pickup time slot during peak hours
- [NF] Mobile app must load queue information within 2 seconds on 4G network
- [F] Staff receives notifications when orders are ready for pickup
- [F] Students receive notifications when their pre-order is ready
